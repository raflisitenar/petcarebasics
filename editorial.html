How we research and maintain our guides

Our aim is to make high-quality pet-care questions easier to understand without pretending that general information replaces individual clinical advice.

Scope and authorship

PetCare Basics publishes independent educational explanations written and edited for clarity. The label “PetCare Basics Editorial” identifies the publishing project, not a claim that a licensed veterinarian personally authored or approved each article. We do not claim to run clinical trials or to have examined particular animals.

Sources and disagreements

For questions about nutrition, vaccination and preventive health we prioritize established professional veterinary guidance, including WSAVA and AAHA. For toxicity and emergency preparedness, we reference organizations such as ASPCA and AVMA. We link to source documents or primary professional guidance wherever practical. Where decisions depend on local regulations or individual risks, we explicitly defer to a qualified local veterinarian.

Original content and useful examples

Guides are organized around specific everyday scenarios. We include household examples, practical observation steps, questions for a veterinarian and a takeaway checklist. Examples are illustrative, not patient cases or endorsements of a treatment. We do not publish unreviewed automated pages or claim original field research.

Corrections and review dates

Pages display the date of the most recent editorial review. When a correction changes the practical meaning of an article, we revise the page and update its modified date. To report an issue, send the page URL and supporting evidence to raflisitenar@gmail.com. A publication date is not proof that every external source has been revised on the same date.

Commercial relationships

Our current guides do not contain sponsored product recommendations or affiliate listings. We may participate in Google AdSense, which can provide contextual or personalized advertising subject to availability and applicable privacy settings. Advertising does not determine our editorial conclusions. See Privacy for more.
