Privacy Policy

Effective date: 6 November 2026. This policy describes the current static website at petcarebasics.vercel.app.

Website information

This is a static educational website. We do not offer user accounts, checkout or a server-side collection form. Hosting infrastructure may process information such as IP addresses, user agents, request paths and access logs for delivery, security and operational purposes, according to the hosting provider’s practices.

Interactive tools

The Daily Care Routine tool stores selection and checkmarks in your own browser’s localStorage so you can resume a checklist on the same browser. The Home Safety Audit processes selections in the browser without sending audit answers to our server. You can clear the stored routine using the tool’s Reset control or browser data settings. Avoid entering private medical or identity details into any tool.

Google and advertising partners may use cookies or similar technologies to serve, limit and measure ads, including personalized advertising when legally permitted and appropriate consent is obtained. Advertiser or Google data collection is governed by their own policies. Depending on visitor location and applicable law, a certified consent-management platform or additional consent steps may be required before certain advertising can operate. This site does not represent that loading the script alone meets every legal requirement.

Learn about Google’s advertising technologies at Google advertising technologies ↗ and Google Privacy Policy ↗.

Email inquiries

If you email raflisitenar@gmail.com, we receive the content you choose to send and your sender address through your email provider. We use this to understand and respond to feedback, correction reports and inquiries. Avoid including unnecessary personal or veterinary records.

Changes and contact

We may update this policy when our features or advertising setup changes. The effective date will be revised. For privacy questions, contact raflisitenar@gmail.com.
