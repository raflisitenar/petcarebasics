Why we avoid medical calculators

A website cannot safely determine medication dose, diagnosis, vaccine schedules or appropriate food amounts for an animal it has not examined. These two tools instead focus on practical organization: remembering everyday activities, identifying home hazards and creating useful questions for a veterinary visit. They are not emergency triage tools.
