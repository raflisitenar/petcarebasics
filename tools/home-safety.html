Why a home audit needs context

A “yes” answer does not certify that a home is safe. Pets differ in climbing, chewing, jumping and opening ability, and visitors can introduce new hazards. Revisit this check when you add furniture or plants, renovate, move house or welcome another animal. Store hazardous chemicals and medicines securely rather than relying on height alone. The ASPCA provides a reference list of human foods that can be harmful to pets, and the AVMA explains how to prepare emergency supplies.
