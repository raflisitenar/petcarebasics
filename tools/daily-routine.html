What should change when your pet changes?

Consider your animal’s appetite, thirst, elimination, mood, grooming, activity and comfort together. A single missed routine item does not diagnose a problem. However, a sudden or persistent change can be important to discuss with a veterinarian. Growing animals need age-appropriate care and preventive visits; senior pets may need easier access and closer observation. This tool deliberately avoids prescribing exact food amounts, exercise minutes or medications.

Use our dog feeding guide, cat feeding guide and veterinary appointment notes to learn how to turn observations into questions. Difficulty breathing, collapse, poisoning, severe trauma or a cat unable to pass urine need urgent veterinary attention, not an online checklist.
