How we use the references

We prioritize veterinary professional guidelines for medical principles, then explain their relevance to a household routine without giving diagnoses or medication doses. For toxic exposures, urgent symptoms, vaccination plans, specific diets or treatment, consult a qualified veterinarian immediately or according to the individual situation. We do not reproduce copyrighted diagrams or tables from professional organizations.

Report a source issue

External pages can move or be revised. Send broken links or factual concerns to raflisitenar@gmail.com, ideally with the affected guide URL. Read our editorial method.
