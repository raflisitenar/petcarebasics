Contact PetCare Basics

For corrections, feedback about a guide, accessibility issues or publication inquiries, email raflisitenar@gmail.com.

What helps with a correction

Include the exact URL, the sentence or subject you believe needs revision, the reason for the concern and, if possible, a reputable reference. We assess submitted corrections editorially; contacting us does not imply a live veterinary consultation or a guaranteed individual response time.

Important: not for urgent pet care

We do not monitor this inbox as an emergency service and cannot diagnose animals or give medication recommendations by email. For acute illness, poisoning, breathing difficulty, collapse, seizures or a cat unable to urinate, contact a qualified veterinarian or local emergency clinic immediately.

Privacy of your message

Please avoid emailing sensitive personal, payment or medical details that are not necessary to explain an editorial issue. General information about our site and third-party services is described in the Privacy Policy.
