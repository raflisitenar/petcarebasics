About PetCare Basics

PetCare Basics is an independently maintained educational project about practical, everyday care for dogs and cats. We organize common owner questions into clear home routines, observations and useful questions to ask qualified professionals.

What we publish

Our library covers new-pet settling, food routines, environmental enrichment, grooming, home safety, life-stage adaptation and preparations for a veterinary visit. Articles are written for owners who want to understand a problem without being pushed toward a product purchase. Each guide includes a specific household example, practical action list and further reading.

What we are not

We are not a veterinary clinic, a licensed veterinarian, an animal poison-control service or a product seller. Our editorial team does not claim to have examined your pet. We cannot diagnose diseases, prescribe medication, determine doses or replace a veterinarian-client-patient relationship. No article on this site should be used to postpone emergency treatment.

Our approach to evidence

We consult established veterinary organizations such as WSAVA and AAHA, alongside specialist animal welfare resources such as ASPCA and AVMA. We summarize in our own words and link to primary guidance wherever feasible. We distinguish universal safety principles from recommendations that depend on species, health, geography and the individual animal.

Contact the publisher

Contact our publisher at raflisitenar@gmail.com for corrections, content concerns or accessibility feedback. Our editorial standards describe how we work, and our source directory explains where technical background comes from.
