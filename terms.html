Educational use only

Our guides provide general pet-care information and ideas for household observation and planning. They are not medical examinations, diagnoses, prescriptions or a replacement for professional veterinary judgment. Nothing on this site establishes a veterinarian-client-patient relationship. Every animal and local regulatory environment is different.

Safety and emergencies

Always consult a qualified veterinarian for illness, injury, medication and vaccination questions. Seek immediate veterinary assistance for serious or rapidly worsening symptoms. Do not use our checklists to decide whether an emergency needs attention.

External sources and changes

We link to third-party resources to help readers verify more technical material. We do not control their content or guarantee that all linked pages remain available. Professional recommendations, product information and local rules may change after publication. Check current information with the responsible source and veterinary team.

Use of tools

Our tools provide nonclinical planning prompts, use your own device and may change. You are responsible for verifying that results suit your circumstances. We cannot guarantee uninterrupted availability or suitability for all visitors. The tools are free and do not ask for an account.
